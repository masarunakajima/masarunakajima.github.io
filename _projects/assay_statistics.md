**Capricor Therapeutics · 2025–present**

I partner with analytical development, QC, and R&D scientists on the statistics behind assays and studies:

- **Experimental design**: mixed-model power and sample-size simulations, including plate-position effects and bridging-study designs.
- **Comparability and equivalence**: TOST equivalence testing with multiplicity control, Welch confidence intervals, stability regression.
- **Trending**: statistical process control with Nelson rules for qPCR standards and controls.
- **Reproducible delivery**: every analysis ships as a frozen package (pinned inputs, scripts, expected outputs, checksums), often with an interactive Streamlit explorer.

**Stack:** Python (statsmodels, SciPy, pandas) · R · Streamlit · SQLite
