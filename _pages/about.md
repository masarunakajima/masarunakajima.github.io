I'm a **bioinformatics scientist** who builds computational methods, pipelines, and data platforms that turn complex biological data into decisions. I work at the intersection of **multi-omics, machine learning, and scalable cloud engineering**.

I am currently a **Senior Bioinformatics Scientist at [Capricor Therapeutics](https://www.capricor.com)** in San Diego, which develops cell and exosome therapeutics. My work there includes:

- **Proteomics and transcriptomics pipelines**: mass-spectrometry proteomics (FragPipe, DIA-NN) and small-RNA workflows, built as reproducible Nextflow pipelines on **AWS HealthOmics**
- **AI/ML for biology**: single-cell foundation models (**scGPT**) deployed on AWS Batch GPU, and LLM-powered tools that extract and rank evidence from the biomedical literature
- **Data platforms**: multi-omics analysis and lab-data applications with ontology-harmonized data models, provenance tracking, and role-based access
- **Statistics for assay development**: experimental design, power simulation, equivalence testing, and trending for analytical development and QC

Previously, I was a **bioinformatics co-op at EMD Serono**. There I used a fine-tuned BioBERT model and GPT-based approaches to map free-text cell annotations to the Cell Ontology (+20% accuracy), and built reproducible single-cell RNA-seq workflows. At **USC's Smith Lab** I was a committer to [DNMTools](https://github.com/smithlabcode/dnmtools) and ran large-scale DNA methylation (WGBS) pipelines.

I hold a **PhD from the University of Southern California** (2023). My dissertation developed the first polynomial-time algorithm for counting distinguishable RNA secondary structures. I also hold an **MSc in Computer Science** (USC). Before that, I studied experimental plasma physics at the University of Saskatchewan (MSc, BSc), where I first learned to love building analysis tools for messy, high-volume data.

**Interests:** multi-omics integration · biological foundation models · single-cell analysis · proteomics · reproducible cloud workflows · biomedical knowledge graphs
